## About The Game
**Thinglings** is an action-fantasy boss battle game centered around a sorcerer in a deep cave who accidentally creates two unique creatures. As these newly born beings, you must navigate challenging encounters, utilizing a unique mechanic where the creatures must bond together and tear apart to survive and ultimately escape their creator.

Featuring beautiful, entirely hand-drawn art, the game emphasizes tight movement, strategic splitting mechanics, and intense boss encounters. 

**Play the game:** [Available now on Itch.io for Windows & macOS](https://firefliesjar.itch.io/thinglings)

## Controls
For the optimal experience, a **wired controller is highly recommended**.
However, keyboard inputs are fully supported:
* **Movement:** `WASD` & `Arrow Keys`
